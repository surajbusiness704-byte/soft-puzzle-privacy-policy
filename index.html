Privacy Policy for Soft Puzzle 2.0

App Name: Soft Puzzle 2.0 | Package: com.true.softpuzzle
Last Updated: September 29, 2026

Thank you for playing Soft Puzzle 2.0 ("the App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a safe and relaxing puzzle gaming experience.

Summary: Soft Puzzle 2.0 does NOT collect personal identifiers (such as your name, physical address, contacts, or photos). We only use standard device telemetry and advertising identifiers to provide game services, save your level progress locally, and reward achievements.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Soft Puzzle 2.0, certain non-personal and gameplay-related information may be processed:

  • Local Game Progress: Your level completions, high scores, lives, hints, and game settings are stored securely on your device using Android Room Database and DataStore.
  • Device and Usage Information: Non-personally identifiable diagnostic information such as device model, OS version, language settings, and crash logs to improve app performance.
  • Advertising & Analytics IDs: Google Advertising ID (AAID) used by Google AdMob for delivering non-intrusive banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads.
  • Reward Tracking Identifier (True Earn UID): If you launch the app via a custom referral or reward deep link (e.g. softpuzzle://play?true_earn_uid=USER_ID), the user ID is securely stored locally on your device solely to trigger automatic postback credit verification when you complete Level 20.

2. Third-Party Services and Advertising

The App integrates trusted third-party SDKs to serve advertisements and analyze system reliability. These providers may collect information in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google AdMob / Google Play Services: Used to display banner ads, interstitial ads, and rewarded video ads for in-game hints and life revives.
    Google Privacy Policy
  • True Earn Postback API: Used exclusively to deliver reward verification for completing Level 20.

3. Permissions Used by the App

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required to load Google AdMob advertisements and verify reward postbacks.
  • android.permission.VIBRATE: Used to provide gentle tactile haptic feedback during arrow moves and button clicks (can be disabled in Settings anytime).

4. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

Soft Puzzle 2.0 is designed for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete such information.

5. Data Security

All communications with ad networks and backend verification endpoints use industry-standard HTTPS encryption. We do not sell, rent, or trade any user data to third parties.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
